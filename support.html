Common starting points

Find the right troubleshooting path.

01

USB mapping

Check Windows game-controller detection first, then map axes and buttons in the simulator profile.

02

Fit and mounting

Send the screen or panel dimensions, fastener pattern and a photo of the current setup.

03

Software integration

Include simulator version, data interface, network layout and the action that is not behaving as expected.

Support questions

Short answers

What should I include in a support message?

Product line, order or quote reference, simulator version, operating system, a short reproduction and any photos or logs that show the problem.

Can you help with a third-party simulator?

We can review a compatibility or integration question, but the final support scope depends on the platform and the interface available to the project.

Where do I send a service question?

Use the contact form or email info@fsdcpak.com with your reference number if you have one.
